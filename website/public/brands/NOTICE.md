# Company logo assets

These logos identify the presenters' employers. They are trademarks of their
respective owners and are not covered by the repository's software license.

- `redhat.svg`: the official Red Hat hat-and-wordmark SVG, extracted from the
  navigation of https://www.redhat.com/en/about/brand/standards/logo.
  The SVG namespace was added so the inline artwork renders as a standalone
  image. Artwork and proportions are unchanged.
- `nvidia.svg`: the official NVIDIA logo symbol from the navigation of
  https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/. The SVG symbol was wrapped as a standalone SVG.
  Artwork, colors, and proportions are unchanged.

The assets are bundled to support offline presentation. Use each with clear
space and preserve its aspect ratio.
